Macro, Weather & Sports Panels

Polymarket questions often depend on data outside Polymarket. These panels bring macro releases, inflation components, commodities, weather, sports, and time-zone context into the same workspace as market prices and fills.

Macro and Inflation Panels

Panel What it shows How to use it
CPI Release Command
cpi-release-command-center		Official release calendar plus nowcast registry for CPI, PCE, and Fed timing.Use it before trading macro markets around release windows and consensus changes.
CPI Components
cpi-components-pressure-registry		Energy, food, shelter, and CPI component pressure registry.Use it to understand which CPI component may be driving headline or core inflation probabilities.
CPI Release Calendar & Consensus Baseline
cpi-release-calendar		Official CPI, PCE, NFP, and FOMC release timing with Polymarket implied CPI baseline.Use it to align market-implied expectations with scheduled official data.
Inflation Nowcast
inflation-nowcast		Cleveland Fed CPI and PCE nowcasting panel.Use it as a neutral nowcast reference when market odds diverge from public model estimates.
Goods / Tariff
goods-tariff-supply-watch		Goods inflation, tariff, import-price, and supply-chain registry.Use it to track tradable goods inflation pressure and policy-driven import cost shocks.
Labor / Services
labor-services-inflation-monitor		Labor, wage, claims, and services CPI pressure registry.Use it to judge whether services inflation risk is coming from wages, labor tightness, or claims data.
Fed / Growth Risk
fed-reaction-growth-risk-board		Fed, rates, growth, demand, and recession-risk registry.Use it to connect inflation prints to Fed reaction and growth-risk markets.
Food & Retail Basket Pressure
food-retail-basket-pressure		Official CPI food-component pressure for inflation market positioning.Use it for food-at-home, food-away-from-home, and retail-basket questions.
Supply / Tariff
supply-tariff-import-watch		Supply-chain and tariff watch for CPI goods pressure.Use it when goods inflation markets depend on shipping, import, tariff, or substitution pressure.
Shelter / OER
shelter-rent-oer-pressure		Rent and owners-equivalent-rent pressure for core CPI markets.Use it to interpret sticky shelter components before official CPI release.
Labor / Wages
labor-wage-services-pressure		Labor-market and wage pressure for services CPI and Fed markets.Use it to connect payroll, wage, and services-price risk in one panel.
Growth / Demand
growth-demand-recession-tracker		Demand and recession indicators for macro market positioning.Use it to evaluate whether macro market odds reflect soft-landing or recession pressure.
Fed / Rates Gap
fed-rates-polymarket-gap		Rates tape and Polymarket Fed-market context.Use it to compare prediction-market pricing with rate-market signals.

Market and Commodity Context

Panel What it shows How to use it
Commodities Watch
commodities-watch		Commodity price boards and sparklines.Use it to anchor energy, metals, food, and inflation markets in live commodity prices.
Energy & Gasoline Shock
energy-gasoline-shock		EIA WTI, gasoline, and diesel pressure for headline CPI markets.Use it when energy-price moves may drive CPI or consumer-cost probabilities.
Global Indices
global-index-monitor		Global index quote board.Use it as broad risk-on/risk-off context for macro and market-direction questions.
Commodity Equity Transmission
commodity-equity-transmission		Commodity shocks mapped into equity beneficiaries, cost-pressure names, spread-watch names, and related Polymarket themes.Use it to translate commodity moves into sector and company implications.
World Clock
world-clock		Live market clocks for Shanghai, New York, London, and the selected weather city.Use it to interpret whether a market is moving during an active session or during thin after-hours liquidity.

Weather Panels

Panel What it shows How to use it
Global Temp Monitor
global-temperature-monitor		Live global city temperatures, forecast highs, and Polymarket quote coverage.Use it as the overview table for weather-linked markets and city-level temperature risk.
Weather Market Browser
weather-market-browser		Grouped Polymarket weather markets across temperature, precipitation, storms, climate, and disaster families.Use it to find the relevant market family before opening a city or quote detail panel.
Weather City Snapshot
weather-city-snapshot		Selected city temperature, condition, daily range, and market coverage.Use it as the city-level weather summary for a focused weather market.
Weather Quote Curve
weather-quote-detail		Selected city Polymarket temperature-bin midpoint curve.Use it to see which temperature outcomes are priced as most likely.
Weather Quote Table
weather-quote-table		Selected city temperature market quote bins in a compact table.Use it for precise bin-level bid, ask, midpoint, and probability comparison.
WU 1 Day
weather-trend-detail		Selected city one-day temperature trend chart.Use it for intraday movement and near-term weather market timing.
WU 7 Day
weather-trend-7d		Selected city seven-day temperature trend chart.Use it to compare short-term market pricing with forecast trend direction.
Weather News
weather-news		City weather headlines from seeded Google News RSS.Use it when market odds may be reacting to storms, heat waves, warnings, or local forecast changes.

Sports Panels

Panel What it shows How to use it
NBA Scoreboard
nba-scoreboard		Upcoming and live NBA games.Use it as the schedule and live-state anchor for NBA-related markets.
NBA Intel
nba-intel		ESPN news, starting lineups, and pregame rumors.Use it to connect lineup or injury information to team and player markets.
ESPN Matchup Predictor
espn-matchup-predictor		ESPN BPI win probability, matchup quality, projected margin, and expected score.Use it as an external model baseline against Polymarket or sportsbook pricing.
Esports Intel
esports-intel		GRID official esports series state with local Polymarket matching context.Use it to verify esports event state and match it back to local market identity.
Sports Odds
sports-odds		Bookmaker consensus monitor with Polymarket comparison hooks.Use it to compare Polymarket probability with external sportsbook consensus.

情报与新闻 Panels

这些 panels 解释 market 为什么可能正在移动，以及它的 resolution path 是否清晰。它们组合 oracle state、related news、AI summaries、policy feeds、finance news、technology signals 和 domain-specific event feeds。

Oracle 与 AI 分析

Panel 显示内容 如何使用
Oracle Feed
oracle-feed		 跨市场近期 oracle events，包含 resolution-adjacent activity。 用于验证 market status 仍由交易驱动，还是已经进入 oracle/resolution phase。
AI Market Brief
price-implications		 全市场 AI brief, focal points, and convergence signals. 用于 compress multiple market signals into a short analyst-readable thesis.
AI Trend Radar
oracle-timeline		 全市场 AI synthesis of trend clusters, catalysts, and watch items. 用于发现围绕同一 catalyst 或 narrative 移动的 related market clusters。
Related News
related-news		 News linked to the focused market. 用于在不离开工作区的情况下，将 probability move 连接到 source、headline 或 external event。

科技与金融情报

Panel 显示内容 如何使用
AI Model Race
ai-model-race		 AI lab release、benchmark、pricing、valuation、outage 和 regulation signal feed。 用于 AI-company 和 model-release markets，尤其是 product news 会快速推动 probability 的场景。
Big Tech Cap
big-tech-market-cap		 Mega-cap technology quote and market-cap rank board. 用于把 tech-market narratives 锚定到 live equity context，而不只是 headline flow。
App Pulse
consumer-app-pulse		 App Store, Google Play, TikTok, consumer ranking, and regulation signals. 用于 adoption、ranking、bans 或 platform changes 重要的 consumer internet markets。
IPO News
ipo-news-watch		 IPO, listing, and filing catalyst feed. 用于 listing, company, valuation, and deal-timing questions.
Broker Research
broker-research-watch		 Analyst rating and target-price change radar. 用于发现可能影响 equity-linked 或 sector-linked markets 的 TradFi analyst catalysts。

政策、风险与事件 feeds

Panel 显示内容 如何使用
Chain Policy
blockchain-policy-news		 Blockchain policy, regulation, and enforcement feed. 用于 crypto regulation, enforcement, ETF, and protocol governance markets.
DeFi Security
defi-security-watch		 DeFi exploit, vulnerability, and protocol risk feed. 用于在更广泛 crypto prices 反映之前识别 protocol-specific security shocks。
Trade Policy
trade-policy-radar		 高信号 trade policy watchlist，映射到 tariffs、flows、barriers、revenue 和 strategic controls。 当 market movement 依赖 duties、export controls、sanctions 或 supply-chain rerouting 时使用。
Geopolitical & Sanctions Shock
geo-sanctions-shock		 Geopolitical shocks, sanctions changes, and linked macro-risk markets. 用于把 global event risk 连接到 commodity、macro、election 和 security markets。
Jin10 Flash
jin10-flash		 Jin10 macro flash feed in market-card form. 用于可能推动 rates、inflation、FX 和 commodity questions 的快速宏观 headline。
BWE News
f1-trackside		 BWENews runtime RSS flashes with cached auto-refresh. 作为紧凑 live news feed，用于需要 headline triage 的 fast-moving event surfaces。

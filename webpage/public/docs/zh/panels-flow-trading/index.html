流动与交易 Panels

这些 panels 解释谁在交易、fills 聚集在哪里、流动性是真实还是稀薄，以及哪些外部交易场所可能正在影响 Polymarket probabilities。它们围绕 OrderFilled rows、wallet flow、cross-market signals，以及 crypto/finance pressure boards 构建。

OrderFilled 流动

Panel 显示内容 如何使用
Orderfilled Flow
global-orderfilled		 跨市场最新链上 OrderFilled activity，包含 market、side、price、size 和 recency context。 作为整个平台的实时 tape 使用，再跳转到正在移动的具体 market。
Whale Tracker
whale-tracker		 近期最大链上 trades，突出 high-notional 或 high-impact wallet behavior。 用于发现仅靠 price history 不明显的集中 flow。
Flow Watch
suspicious-flow		 值得额外复核的 oracle-adjacent 与 large live trade flow。 适用于 market 临近 close、resolution，或因异常 fill activity 移动时。
PolySignal
polybeats-feed		 Smart-money flow、wallet history 和可解释 market briefs。 用于把 wallet activity 连接到可读 market thesis，而不是只盯着原始地址。

信号 Panels

Panel 显示内容 如何使用
Alpha Signal
alpha-signal		 跨来源 heuristic signal stack，组合 price action、flow、external content 和 market context。 作为 triage layer 使用：它不替代研究，但能告诉你哪些 markets 最值得先看。
AI Special Markets
sample-chain-trades		 全市场 AI radar for unusual, high-attention, and fast-moving markets. 用于 find special situations that are not captured by a single category filter.

Crypto 与金融流动

Panel 显示内容 如何使用
Crypto Watch
crypto-watch		 Crypto spot trends and sparklines across major assets. 适用于 crypto prices are likely to explain probability movement in crypto-linked Polymarket markets.
Crypto Funding Watch
crypto-funding-watch		 Cross-venue perpetual funding heatmap with long/short crowding bias. 用于 identify crowded positioning before it appears in prediction-market odds.
Crypto Perps
crypto-perp-funding		 Crypto perpetual funding pressure list. 可作为 a compact ranking of markets where derivatives positioning is most stretched.
TradFi Perps
tradfi-perp-radar		 Tokenized stock, index, and commodity perpetual radar. 用于 connect synthetic-market pressure to Polymarket questions about equities, indices, and commodities.
Crypto ETF
crypto-etf-flow		 BTC and ETH ETF flow proxy board. 用于 institutional flow context around Bitcoin, Ethereum, and ETF-related event markets.
Fear & Greed
crypto-fear-greed		 Crypto sentiment gauge with price drivers. 用于 distinguish broad sentiment pressure from market-specific news.
Stablecoins
stablecoin-monitor		 Stablecoin peg and supply monitor. 适用于 market movement depends on crypto liquidity, depeg risk, or stablecoin supply changes.
DeFi Token Watch
defi-token-watch		 Compact DeFi token tape with price, 24h move, and weekly trend. 用于 monitor protocol tokens that may affect DeFi, governance, or exploit-related markets.
DeFi Yields
defi-yield-monitor		 Protocol APY and TVL watch board. 用于 track yield stress, capital rotation, and protocol-level risk appetite.

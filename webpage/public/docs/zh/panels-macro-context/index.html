宏观、天气与体育 Panels

Polymarket 问题经常依赖 Polymarket 之外的数据。这些 panels 将宏观发布、通胀分项、大宗商品、天气、体育和时区上下文带入与 market prices 和 fills 相同的工作区。

宏观与通胀 Panels

Panel 显示内容 如何使用
CPI 发布指挥面板
cpi-release-command-center		CPI、PCE 和 Fed timing 的官方 release calendar 与 nowcast registry。用于在 trading macro markets around release windows and consensus changes.
CPI 分项
cpi-components-pressure-registry		Energy, food, shelter, and CPI component pressure registry.用于理解哪个 CPI component 可能正在驱动 headline 或 core inflation probabilities。
CPI Release Calendar & Consensus Baseline
cpi-release-calendar		官方 CPI、PCE、NFP 和 FOMC release timing，并带 Polymarket implied CPI baseline。用于 align market-implied expectations with scheduled official data.
Inflation Nowcast
inflation-nowcast		Cleveland Fed CPI and PCE nowcasting panel.当 market odds 偏离公开模型估计时，作为中性 nowcast reference。
Goods / Tariff
goods-tariff-supply-watch		Goods inflation, tariff, import-price, and supply-chain registry.用于追踪 tradable goods inflation pressure 和政策驱动的 import cost shocks。
Labor / Services
labor-services-inflation-monitor		Labor, wage, claims, and services CPI pressure registry.用于判断 services inflation risk 是否来自 wages、labor tightness 或 claims data。
Fed / Growth Risk
fed-reaction-growth-risk-board		Fed, rates, growth, demand, and recession-risk registry.用于 connect inflation prints to Fed reaction and growth-risk markets.
Food & Retail Basket Pressure
food-retail-basket-pressure		用于 inflation market positioning 的官方 CPI food-component pressure。用于 food-at-home, food-away-from-home, and retail-basket questions.
Supply / Tariff
supply-tariff-import-watch		Supply-chain and tariff watch for CPI goods pressure.当 goods inflation markets 依赖 shipping、import、tariff 或 substitution pressure 时使用。
Shelter / OER
shelter-rent-oer-pressure		Rent and owners-equivalent-rent pressure for core CPI markets.用于 interpret sticky shelter components before official CPI release.
Labor / Wages
labor-wage-services-pressure		Labor-market and wage pressure for services CPI and Fed markets.用于 connect payroll, wage, and services-price risk in one panel.
Growth / Demand
growth-demand-recession-tracker		Demand and recession indicators for macro market positioning.用于评估 macro market odds 反映的是 soft-landing 还是 recession pressure。
Fed / Rates Gap
fed-rates-polymarket-gap		Rates tape and Polymarket Fed-market context.用于 compare prediction-market pricing with rate-market signals.

市场与大宗商品上下文

Panel 显示内容 如何使用
Commodities Watch
commodities-watch		Commodity price boards and sparklines.用于把 energy、metals、food 和 inflation markets 锚定到 live commodity prices。
Energy & Gasoline Shock
energy-gasoline-shock		EIA WTI, gasoline, and diesel pressure for headline CPI markets.适用于 energy-price moves may drive CPI or consumer-cost probabilities.
Global Indices
global-index-monitor		Global index quote board.可作为 broad risk-on/risk-off context for macro and market-direction questions.
Commodity Equity Transmission
commodity-equity-transmission		将 commodity shocks 映射到 equity beneficiaries、cost-pressure names、spread-watch names 和相关 Polymarket themes。用于 translate commodity moves into sector and company implications.
World Clock
world-clock		上海、纽约、伦敦和选中天气城市的 live market clocks。用于判断 market 是在 active session 中移动，还是在 after-hours 薄流动性中移动。

天气 Panels

Panel 显示内容 如何使用
Global Temp Monitor
global-temperature-monitor		全球城市实时温度、forecast highs 和 Polymarket quote coverage。作为 weather-linked markets 和 city-level temperature risk 的概览表。
Weather Market Browser
weather-market-browser		按 temperature、precipitation、storms、climate 和 disaster families 分组的 Polymarket weather markets。用于在打开 city 或 quote detail panel 前找到相关 market family。
Weather City Snapshot
weather-city-snapshot		Selected city temperature, condition, daily range, and market coverage.可作为 the city-level weather summary for a focused weather market.
Weather Quote Curve
weather-quote-detail		Selected city Polymarket temperature-bin midpoint curve.用于 see which temperature outcomes are priced as most likely.
Weather Quote Table
weather-quote-table		Selected city temperature market quote bins in a compact table.用于 precise bin-level bid, ask, midpoint, and probability comparison.
WU 1 Day
weather-trend-detail		Selected city one-day temperature trend chart.用于 intraday movement and near-term weather market timing.
WU 7 Day
weather-trend-7d		Selected city seven-day temperature trend chart.用于 compare short-term market pricing with forecast trend direction.
Weather News
weather-news		City weather headlines from seeded Google News RSS.当 market odds 可能对 storms、heat waves、warnings 或 local forecast changes 反应时使用。

体育 Panels

Panel 显示内容 如何使用
NBA Scoreboard
nba-scoreboard		Upcoming and live NBA games.可作为 the schedule and live-state anchor for NBA-related markets.
NBA Intel
nba-intel		ESPN news, starting lineups, and pregame rumors.用于 connect lineup or injury information to team and player markets.
ESPN Matchup Predictor
espn-matchup-predictor		ESPN BPI win probability、matchup quality、projected margin 和 expected score。可作为 an external model baseline against Polymarket or sportsbook pricing.
Esports Intel
esports-intel		GRID 官方 esports series state，并带本地 Polymarket matching context。用于 verify esports event state and match it back to local market identity.
Sports Odds
sports-odds		Bookmaker consensus monitor with Polymarket comparison hooks.用于 compare Polymarket probability with external sportsbook consensus.
